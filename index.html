Project Positioning

A practical first step before AI floor-plan recognition.

Instead of beginning with high-cost automatic architectural drawing analysis, the first phase focuses on a usable, testable, and expandable questionnaire platform. The product translates professional feng shui topics into visual prompts, everyday language, and a structured assessment flow that general users can complete with confidence.

Phase 1 Scope

Core features for a believable assessment platform.

01

Interactive Visual Questionnaire

Simplified questions with scene-based illustrations help users identify spatial relationships, home layout conditions, and key feng shui cues.

02

Account and Progress Saving

Registration, login, saved progress, historical records, and basic result storage create the foundation for future subscriptions and paid services.

03

Initial Score and Suggestions

Users receive an overall score and early-stage guidance that frames feng shui as a reflection on comfort, mood, balance, and daily living quality.

04

Consultation Entry Point

A contact page connects users with professional consultants, researchers, or service partners after they complete the first assessment.

Contract Addendum

Proposal addendum and delivery scope confirmation.

The following items are included in the website development and launch delivery scope to clarify ownership, account management, change requests, warranty responsibilities, SEO / analytics tracking services, and excluded items.

01

Intellectual Property and Code Ownership

Upon full payment, STOA / the client will receive ownership or usage rights to the final website deliverables as defined in the agreement, including website design, content, custom interface design, front-end code, specifications, and delivery files.

Client Ownership Scope

  • Website visual design output, content, and copy
  • Custom interface design and front-end code
  • Project feature logic, design files, specifications, and delivery files

Items to Exclude or List Clearly

  • Development frameworks, libraries, and plugins
  • Reusable generic code or underlying technologies
02

Infrastructure and Account Ownership

All external infrastructure and third-party service accounts will be registered and managed under STOA / the client's company name, with payment information tied to STOA / the client's company payment method.

Management Principles

  • The client retains top-level permissions such as Owner or Super Admin
  • Developers receive only the technical access required for implementation
  • GCP, domain, and external service accounts are owned by the paying party

Included Accounts

  • Domain, hosting / server, DNS, and GCP accounts
  • Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, and other third-party services
03

Design Approval and Change Requests

Once the website design has been reviewed and approved by the client, the approved design shall serve as the basis for subsequent website development and implementation.

Minor Adjustments After Approval

  • Text edits, image replacements, and color refinements
  • Spacing adjustments and minor layout corrections
  • Updates that do not affect the overall structure or design direction

Major Changes or Additional Requests

  • Requests estimated to require more than three hours of additional work
  • Changes to visual direction, page structure, key user flow, or layouts
  • New pages, new features, or work outside the agreed project scope
  • Separate quotation, revised timeline, and written approval may be required
04

Maintenance and Warranty

After official launch, the project includes a three-month warranty period for issues related to the delivered website implementation.

Warranty Scope

  • Free fixes for code errors and development bugs
  • Free corrections for delivered feature issues and layout errors
  • Warranty applies to the approved and delivered project scope

Items Outside Warranty

  • New features, new pages, or redesigned layouts after launch
  • Third-party service outages, policy changes, or subscription issues
05

SEO Service Scope

SEO is included in the website development and launch delivery scope to ensure the site has the foundation for search visibility and ongoing data analysis.

Basic SEO Setup

  • Google index readability, URL structure, and Schema.org structured data

  • Google Search Console integration

Analytics Tracking

  • Google Analytics 4 setup
  • Conversion tracking and event tracking setup
  • Long-term SEO, advertising, or advanced analytics can be scoped separately
06

Out of Scope

The following items are not included in the current project scope unless separately quoted and confirmed by both parties.

SEO and Marketing Exclusions

  • Long-term SEO ranking guarantee
  • Monthly SEO content strategy or keyword reports
